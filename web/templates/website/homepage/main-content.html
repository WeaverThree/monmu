Canal City Stories

Canal City Stories is a post-apocalyptic Sci-Fi roleplaying environment set in a future where the planet has been scorched and the only remaining population lives deep underground in massive cave complexes. Humans are as distant a legend as the surface. The people here are all pokemorphs, engineered in the forgotten past from both man and ‘mon. Amidst resource pressure and the very human elements of greed, lust, and revenge, under a glinting cavern “sky,” you must find your way. Are you an adventurer of the Guild? One of the scattered but resilient rogues? A merc out to make cash above all else? Will you heed the call of the mystery dungeon? How will you leave your mark on the underground?
